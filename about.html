About Us

India's Fastest Growing Non Woven Manufacturer

Maruti Textiles has rapidly emerged as one of India's most dynamic and trusted names in the nonwoven fabric industry. With cutting-edge manufacturing technology and an unwavering focus on quality, we create high-performance PP spunbond fabrics for healthcare, agriculture, hygiene, packaging and industrial applications.

Our growth is guided by innovation, operational efficiency and customer satisfaction. We work closely with businesses across India and beyond to deliver dependable fabric solutions that help them scale confidently in a demanding market.

Why Maruti

  • Strong manufacturing discipline with a consistent quality-first approach.
  • Reliable production capacity and dependable lead times for growing demands.

  • Customer-focused support from product development through delivery.
