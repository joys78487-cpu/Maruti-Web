Our Journey

From beginning to present

Maruti's journey reflects steady growth, strong vision and a commitment to excellence in nonwoven manufacturing.

2010

Foundation

Maruti Textiles was incorporated in 2010 and began its journey in the nonwoven fabric industry.

2011

First Production Line

Installed and commissioned the 1.6 S line, introducing nonwoven fabric into the North India market.

2016

Second Line

Added a second 1.6 S spunbond line to meet growing demand and improve production capacity.

2018

Slitting Capability

Added a slitting machine to produce precise roll widths and meet varied customer requirements.

2021

Lamination

Introduced lamination capability to strengthen fabric and support customer specific applications.

2023

Printing

Added a modern printing line to serve the growing demand for branded and printed nonwoven products.
